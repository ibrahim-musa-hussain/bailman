Who we are

Bailman Global Group is the proposed umbrella brand for a diversified group of businesses. It is being built step by step around international trade and cross-border partnerships.

The group’s existing registered company is Bailman Global Market Limited, incorporated in Nigeria on 13 May 2026 under registration number RC 9542210. Bailman Global Group itself is not a registered holding company. Other divisions are in development.

Our vision

To build a diversified and trusted international business group connecting markets, people and opportunities.

Our mission

To develop sustainable businesses, facilitate international trade and create mutually beneficial partnerships across markets.

Our approach to international business

We start with your requirement: the product, the quantity and the market you serve. We then identify suitable suppliers, communicate clearly about what is and is not possible, and help coordinate the trade.

We are committed to transparency, trust, quality and long-term partnerships. We do not publish invented statistics, reviews or certifications. When we have something to show, we will show it.
